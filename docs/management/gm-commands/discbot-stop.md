## `.minchatlevel`

- Security: Administrator (4)
- Console: yes
- Handler: `HandleMinChatLevelCommand`

Syntax:

```text
.minchatlevel <minlevel>
```

Example:

```text
.minchatlevel 1
```

- Security: Developer (3)
- Console: no
- Handler: `HandlePvPCommand`

Syntax:

```text
.pvp <on|off>
```

Example:

```text
.pvp on
```
